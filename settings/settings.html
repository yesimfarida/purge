Protection

Strict mode adds friction: removing a site or turning off protection means typing a confirmation phrase first. It can't make Purge impossible to bypass, since you own your browser, but it gives you a moment to think.

Chrome doesn't run extensions in Incognito windows by default. To block there too, open chrome://extensions, click Details on Purge, and turn on "Allow in Incognito".

Your data

Everything is stored only in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
